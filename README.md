# 🕷️ Spider-Man: No Way Home

## Overview
Spider-Man: No Way Home is a 2021 superhero film from the *Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)*.  
It follows Peter Parker as he deals with the chaos caused by his identity being revealed to the world.

---

## Main Cast
- 🕸️ Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man 
- 💫 Zendaya as MJ 
- 🧠 Benedict Cumberbatch as Doctor Strange 
- 😈 Willem Dafoe as Green Goblin
- ⚡ Jamie Foxx as Electro 

---

## **Plot Summary**
> The multiverse is real — and it’s breaking open.”  
> — Doctor Strange

After his secret identity is exposed, Peter Parker asks **Doctor Strange** to cast a spell to make people forget.  
However, things go wrong, and villains from **other universes** appear — bringing chaos, danger, and emotional moments.

---

### Key Themes
1. Responsibility – Peter learns that being a hero means making tough choices.  
2. Sacrifice – He gives up everything to protect others.  
3. Redemption – Former villains get a chance to change.
